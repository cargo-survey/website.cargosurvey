Photo evidence for marine cargo surveys, organised from the quayside to the claim.

CargoSurvey helps marine surveyors capture, file and share cargo condition evidence. Every photograph is linked to the right vessel, survey and shipment, and kept as a reliable record for insurers and cargo owners.

Get in touch

What it does

One place for the photographs and observations that decide a cargo claim.

Capture on site

Surveyors photograph cargo on their phones, even without signal. Photos upload when the connection returns.

Send by WhatsApp

Surveyors can also send photos to our WhatsApp number. They choose the shipment, and each photo is filed against it.

Evidence you can rely on

Each photograph is fingerprinted when it arrives and can't be quietly edited or deleted afterwards. A full audit trail records who saw or changed what.

Shared with the right people

Insurers and cargo owners see only the surveys and cargo they're entitled to, through their own secure portal.

Who it's for

Built with working marine surveyors, for everyone who relies on their findings.

  • Marine surveyorsLess time sorting photos, more time on the vessel.
  • Insurers and P&I clubsOrganised, trustworthy evidence for each claim.
  • Cargo owners and receiversA clear record of the condition of their cargo.
